# Roadmaps

## Public roadmap

You must publish your team's roadmap on a publicly accessible URL so that NHS staff in ICBs, trusts and GP surgeries are kept informed about what is coming in the NHS App.

Your public roadmap should talk about the outcomes being enabled by the work you are doing. If helpful (and if known) you can also describe the outputs.

Your public roadmap should cover things that have recently been delivered and planned work for the next 6-12 months. The standard format we use is:

- Recently delivered: outputs from previous quarter
- Now: outcomes being worked on during the current quarter
- Next: outcomes you are planning to work on in the next quarter
- Later: outcomes you are planning to work on in the next 2 quarters after that
