# NHS App Playbook

> **THIS PLAYBOOK IS A WORK IN PROGRESS**
>
> The content on this site is not final and may change without notice. We are actively working on revisions. Do not treat it as complete or accurate yet. We welcome feedback and contributions to help us improve it!

This playbook sets out how teams must design, build, launch, and run services within the NHS App.

The NHS App has over 13 million monthly active users. It is a shared platform: the NHS App team acts as Landlord, and NHS England service teams act as Tenants, each owning the features they ship. The playbook explains the rules of that relationship and the practices, tools, and reference material teams need to work effectively within it.

## Who this is for

This playbook is for teams who are:

- building services that live in the NHS App codebase
- building authenticated web integrations accessible through the NHS App
- contributing design, technical, product, or delivery practices for NHS App services

---

## How the playbook is organised

The playbook is organised by **document type**, not by profession, so it can scale as new design, technical, product management, and delivery management content is added.

- **[Guides](guides/README.md)** contain active, prescriptive guidance on how we work.
- **[References](references/README.md)** contain factual, contextual, and decision-support material.
- **[Open discussions](open-discussions/README.md)** contain early-stage thinking and positions that are still being shaped.
- **[Archive](archive/README.md)** contains historical drafts and workshop material kept for context.

Where another source is already the canonical place for a standard, policy, or live service resource, we link to it rather than duplicating it here.
